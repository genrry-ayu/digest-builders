基础设施

Vercel 的 AI Gateway 正在成为模型层的"内容分发网络"

Guillermo Rauch 将 AI Gateway 定义为"Token Delivery Network"——一个面向 AI 模型的 CDN。其核心能力是动态流量重写：当模型被弃用（如 Fable 退役），企业无需重新部署即可即时切换路由。这是一个被大多数人低估的信号：AI 基础设施正在从"单模型接入"走向"多模型编排+流量治理"。Vercel 在押注的不是某一款模型，而是模型之间的调度层本身。

▶ 查看原文
We've been explaining AI Gateway as a Content Delivery Network. Like a CDN for AI models. One great feature of CDNs is the ability to dynamically re-route or deny traffic without redeployment. When Fable was suddenly retired, we worried about production workloads depending on it. Yet our data shows people still having happy production traffic of older model versions! We're solving this now with AI Gateway Rules. e.g.: you can now rewrite model 'routes' on the fly!

— Guillermo Rauch (Vercel CEO) 原文

产业信号

企业 AI 部署的"最后一公里"是 FDE——不是更好的模型

Box CEO Aaron Levie 给出了本轮 AI 周期中最清醒的论断：让 AI Agent 可靠地进入企业工作流，需要清理数据、现代化 IT 系统、构建评估体系、推动变革管理——所有这些都比模型本身更难。他说"大多数工作流在设计时并没有为 AI Agent 预留位置"，而"FDE（全栈 AI 部署工程师）将成为科技行业最重要的职位之一"。这不是悲观，而是产业成熟度的真实写照。

▶ 查看原文
The deployment of AI in the enterprise beyond just interacting with a chatbot will unequivocally take real work to align AI systems to the underlying business processes they're involved in and drive the desired outcomes. Most workflows weren't designed for AI agents to just drop into. Workflows today deal with fragmented data, legacy software systems that agents can't connect with, institutional instead of documented knowledge, and more. To deploy agents reliably at scale you need to get data cleaned up, modernize IT systems, figure out evals, drive change management. This is why the FDE role will be one of the most critical jobs in tech going forward.

— Aaron Levie (Box CEO) 原文

经济模型

AI 的能源需求正在催生新一代核反应堆的"硬件迭代"逻辑

No Priors 与 Valar Atomics 创始人 Isaiah Taylor 的对话带来了一个跨越两个行业的洞察：AI 的能源需求不仅是一个供给问题，更是核工业的"福特时刻"。Taylor 的核心论点是核工业需要在硬件迭代中重生——从"设计完美的纸面反应堆"转向"制造并运行真实反应堆"。Valar 的理念是从制造一万台"丰田凯美瑞"级别的反应堆开始，而不是追逐一台完美的"兰博基尼"。他们已经让一台 250kW 反应堆在 DOE 授权下临界发电——这是 50 多年来美国首台投入运行的 Triso 反应堆。

▶ 查看原文
Valor is in the camp that we believe this is a hardware execution problem. This is really an issue of can you build reactors? Can you turn them on? Can you operate them well? Can you go from making one to 10 to a 100 to a thousand? The problem of nuclear today is like the Toyota Camry problem. We don't wanna make Lamborghinis. We want to make a very simple, very cheap, very safe reactor that we can make literally tens of thousands of.

— Isaiah Taylor (Valar Atomics 创始人) 完整播客
