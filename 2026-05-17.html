AI 时代的 PM：要么发明新玩法，要么被淘汰

Google PM Madhu Guru 这段话值得每一个做产品的人停下来想三秒："过去二十年，少数团队发明产品范式，其他人把它们搬到自己领域——这足够建立职业生涯，但也让所有软件长得一样。AI 没有稳定范式可抄。你不能靠 A/B 测试做出一款突破性 AI 产品。"Guillermo Rauch 用另一句话印证了同一逻辑："如果你管理 Agent 的能力出类拔萃，但同时对你的基本功也有着深刻的理解，你会势不可挡。"两条线索指向同一个判断：在 AI 时代，可复制性消失了，个人判断力和真正的框架创新能力正在重获溢价。

企业策略

一个公司级超级 Agent，好过每人一个个人 Agent

Dan Shipper 这周分享了他基于 OpenClaw 搭建 Agent-as-a-Service 平台的一手经验，结论很诚实但也很有价值：在目前阶段，一个公司级的超级 Agent 优于每人一个个人 Agent。原因是——Agent 需要持续的技术维护，有本职工作的人不会整天折腾 Agent 的内部配置。这个发现对企业的 AI 部署策略有直接指导意义：与其全员铺开个人 Agent，不如先培养一个能深度维护超级 Agent 的"Agent 工程师"角色。同时也说明 Agent 平台层还有巨大的产品空间——让非技术人员也能轻松管理自己的 Agent。

▶ 查看原文
"OpenClaw is awesome but it's EXTREMELY hard to build on it as a platform. it moves super fast, there are tons of regressions, it's not great to be the layer in between OpenClaw and a user. One super agent for a company beats 1-1 agents for everyone. I do think we're going to get there over time, but for now agents actually require a lot of work (often technical) to keep working well. And people with jobs don't want to be messing with the internals of the agent all day."
— Dan Shipper, Every CEO（来源）
技术路线

Yann LeCun 的 AGI 路线图：LLM 是一条死路，JEPA 才是正解

Yann LeCun 在 Unsupervised Learning 播客上火力全开——他说 LLM "本质上不安全"、"无法被修复"、"不可能达到人类智能"。他创立的 Ami Labs 正在押注 JEPA（联合嵌入预测架构）作为世界模型的实现路径。核心逻辑是：预测像素是死路，应该预测抽象表征。这和 OpenAI/Anthropic 路线的分歧不是渐进式的，而是根本性的：他认为 LLM 只能处理"语言作为推理基座"的领域（数学、代码），但无法理解物理世界。五年内实现"完全世界统治"——这是他给的 timeline。不管你是否同意他的判断，这是目前对 LLM 路线最尖锐、最有体系的质疑之一。

▶ 查看原文
"LLMs are intrinsically unsafe. I don't think they can be made reliable and safe. They cannot be made reliable because you can't stop them from hallucinating. And if they are agentic, you cannot guarantee they're not going to take an action that they didn't predict the outcome of."
— Yann LeCun, NYU 教授 / Ami Labs 创始人（来源）
"There's nothing wrong with LLMs in the sense of LLMs are the basis for a lot of very useful AI products. They're great for what they do. They're just not a path towards human level or human-like intelligence or even animal-like intelligence."
— Yann LeCun（来源）
"What you need is a platform which basically is an open free foundation model, LLM style, that is fine-tunable by anyone to cater to the interest of people speaking a particular language, having a particular culture, having particular value systems."

Aaron Levie

levie — Box CEO

I'm fully forward deployed engineering pilled specifically because AI simply is not the same as software. In software, you deliver a stable piece of technology to a customer and they adopt it and that's that. In AI, you're delivering something that is constantly evolving both due to the nature of the new capabilities and best practices that emerge, but also because the underlying models change so much that they can meaningfully change the workflow as a result of their upgrades. For this reason it's far more logical that one vendor can share best practices across thousands of companies more efficiently than every single company can learn and manage these best practices themselves. Further, the learnings from those customers should go right back into the core product as a result. As we go from chat systems to agentic systems that require more meaningful efforts to manage and update, the FDE model (or equivalent) essentially becomes a core competency for anyone deploying AI at scale.

Unsupervised Learning（Ep 86）— Yann LeCun

2026.05.15 · Redpoint AI

核心论点：Yann LeCun 在离开 Meta 创立 Ami Labs 后首次深度阐述了完整的 JEPA 路线图。他直言 LLM 是"本质上不安全"的架构，预测像素是一条死路。他主张的替代方案是 JEPA（联合嵌入预测架构）——不预测像素，而是预测抽象表征。他认为未来五年内世界模型将取代表面感知模型，并在采访中放出豪言："五年内完全世界统治"。

Tapestry 项目：LeCun 同时推进了一个平行项目 Tapestry——通过联邦学习式的参数向量交换来构建真正开放的基础模型，让非美非中的国家拥有 AI 主权。"OpenAI、Anthropic 就是九十年代的 Sun Microsystems 和 HP-UX，Linux 终将取代它们。"
