范式迁移
"模型吃掉脚手架" 不是未来，是正在发生的现实
Logan Kilpatrick 在 Training Data 播客中提到一个关键判断：今天我们称之为"模型"的东西，早已不再是纯粹的一组权重——它包含了搜索、代码执行、工具调用等越来越厚的系统层。当某个能力层从外部工具变成模型原生，对应中间层的商业价值就会被抽空。这对创业者的启示是：不要押注于短期内被模型能力吞咽的中间件层，比如通用的 agent harness。真正的护城河在垂直领域内：你懂客户、懂数据、懂问题边界，而模型公司为了覆盖广度会主动放弃这种深度。
▶ 查看原文
"The scaffolding is oftentimes a couple of steps ahead of what is baked directly into the model, and then what ends up happening is the model eats that scaffolding and it becomes part of the native model system. It does feel like maybe the agent harness is the quintessential example of this right now, where everyone's like, we gotta go build a harness, and the harness is where the alpha is. I think that perhaps won't be true, at least in the way that we think of the harness today in twelve months."
— Logan Kilpatrick · Training Data 播客
