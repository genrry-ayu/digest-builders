洞察 Insights

能力模型

专家 + AI > 新手 + AI：深度能力的护城河正在加深

当所有人在讨论"vibe coding"和"提示词就够了"时，Box CEO Aaron Levie 发出了一个清醒的、值得反复咀嚼的反信号。他的核心判断是：AI 不会抹平能力差距，反而会放大它。

"The expert with AI is always going to be far more capable than the novice."——这句话值得每一个 Builder 记在墙上。能理解系统架构的人用 Agent 重构十年遗留代码，懂产品的人用 AI 自动化全链路增长实验，有行业知识的人用 Agent 搭建专业工作流。AI 是杠杆的加长，不是杠杆的替代。

这在能力市场意味着什么？对于个人，深耕专业领域 + AI 工具链是当前回报率最高的职业策略。对于团队，用 AI 替代 junior 岗位可能是个危险的陷阱——你真正需要的是 senior + AI，而不是 AI + prompt。

▸ 查看原文
One of the best things students and colleges can do is not bail on learning and teaching the fundamentals of any given domain. AI will trick you into thinking you don't need to go deep in a particular area, but that's wrong.

The expert with AI is always going to be far more capable than the novice. Those that can steer AI agents properly, figure out how to evaluate their work, fix their mistakes, and incorporate their work into a workflow will always be the most potent users of these tools.

The experienced software developer that's built and scaled complex systems using agents outrun someone just vibe coding. The designer that uses AI will build far better products and campaigns than anyone else. The banker or analyst that understands financial models will be able to pull off far more with agents.

Despite some of the rhetoric in the valley that this is less implement now, that couldn't be further from the case. Don't give up on going deep in your craft.
— Aaron Levie, Box CEO · 原文链接
市场信号

AI 不是岗位的终结者，而是人才需求的放大器

和"AI 取代程序员"的主流叙事相反，Levie 抛出了一个更真实也更具反差的观察：AI 让编程变得丰富，但每一个人都需要技术人才来实现 Agent 系统。CIO 和 CEO 们——从科技公司到制药巨头——不是在裁员，而是在疯狂抢人。

他认为 CS 毕业生的传统流向（进大厂做 C 端软件）已经不够用了。"当 AI 使编码变得丰富时，实现 Agent 系统的技术人才需求会激增。"这不是乐观鸡汤，而是他从 Fortune 500 CEO 那里直接听到的第一手信号。

背后的逻辑值得拆解：自动化从来不是去掉人类，而是重新定义人类的位置。AI 降低的是"写代码"的门槛，但架构设计、系统理解、Agent 编排、结果评估这些高阶能力反而变得更加稀缺。

▸ 查看原文
Right now there's a temporary mismatch between the jobs that used to be sought after in some fields and the new jobs that are becoming in demand in those fields.

If you studied CS, for years the general direction of travel was often to join a tech company and build customer-facing software. A significant portion of the CS pipeline from college to hire was built for this.

When you realize that AI is going to make coding abundant, you realize everyone will need technical talent to implement agentic systems. This means the types of roles engineers should be thinking about radically expands.

I was talking to a Fortune 500 pharma CEO a week ago that commented on how much more technical talent they need right now. And this is what I'm hearing from every CIO and CEO across nearly every industry right now.

如果说前几个月 Agent 还是个 demo 概念，本周来自 Alephic 联合创始人 Noah 的一线实践，揭示了 Agent 已经进入日常生活的深度。

最震撼的一个细节：他在池塘边坐着，用手机打开 Claude Code，发现生产环境 bug → 定位问题 → 推送修复 → 创建 PR。"And I was still sitting outside by the pond."——这个场景不是 demo，这是日常。

创业战略

建造变容易了，但注意力是新的护城河

本周一个跨越多个 Builder 的共鸣信号：AI 让建造变得前所未有的容易，但真正的稀缺资源已经不是代码，而是注意力。

Zara Zhang 精准地指出了这一点："人们总是高估建造的难度，低估赢得注意力的难度。"——在 AI 代码生成的时代，这句话的权重翻倍了。

Garry Tan 的回应更激进：他直接怼 Ken Griffin 的"降本"逻辑，认为天花板被 AI 抬高了，真正应该做的是"烧干海洋"（boil the ocean），而不是降低成本。一个会用 AI 构建人机协同团队的 20 多岁年轻人，完全可能颠覆整个对冲基金。

两句话放在一起，画出了一个清晰的战略图景：建造的门槛在变成零的同时，分发、品牌、社区粘性和注意力本身成为了新的护城河。

人才市场

独角兽的股权陷阱：纸面富贵正在变成韭菜收割

FPV Ventures 合伙人 Nikunj Kothari 发布了一条可能会影响很多人职业决策的预警：当前明星独角兽公司的股权结构正在出现一个系统性风险——tranched valuation（分层估值）。

核心问题在于：新员工的入股价往往是 lead investor 优先股价格的 2 倍以上。这意味着纸上看起来惊人的"大包裹"，实际行权价格极高。如果公司在下次融资前增长不够，员工手里的期权可能一文不值。加上 409A 估值的上调，早期行权也要付一大笔税。

他建议每个考虑加入独角兽的技术人才："用 Claude/ChatGPT 算一下你的期权退出数学，像投资人一样做尽职调查。"在 AI 驱动的裁员和估值调整周期中，这可能是今年最实用的一条职业建议。

▸ 查看原文
Employees joining some recently minted unicorn companies are facing a triple whammy:

1) Tranched valuations are very common now — your entry strike price at that valuation is often >2x of the lead preferred price.
2) Your strike price will be quite high and so will be your 409a. You'll be paying a pretty penny if you try to early exercise.
3) Stock packages seem great 'on paper' but are massively inflated because of this tranched valuation.

Make sure you go with wide eyes and use Claude/ChatGPT to do some exit math on your equity.

Joining a unicorn company can offer far more than just comp (learning, experience, strong mission, great team) and that should be the core reason for joining. But do your own due diligence on the company you're joining just like an investor would.
— Nikunj Kothari, Partner @ FPV Ventures · 原文链接
