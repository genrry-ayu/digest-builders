Training Data ElevenLabs 联合创始人 Mati Staniszewski

核心观点：语音正在成为一切交互的界面

Mati Staniszewski 和他的高中好友 Piotr 在 2022 年创立了 ElevenLabs。灵感来自波兰一个独特的文化现象：所有外语电影都用同一个单调旁白配音。这让他们意识到，让每个人用相同情感和语调说任何语言，是一个巨大的未解问题。

"我们从小就在这种环境中长大，今天大多数内容仍然是这样的体验。这让我们看到了音频领域的一个清晰方向：让每个人都能用相同的情感、相同的语调说任何语言。"

ElevenLabs 从语音克隆起步，逐步扩展到配音、音乐、音效生成。Mati 认为语音将成为 AI 交互的主要界面，而不仅仅是文本的附属品。

This works really well btw, at the end of your query ask your LLM to "structure your response as HTML", then view the generated file in your browser. I've also had some success asking the LLM to present its output as slideshows, etc.

More generally, imo audio is the human-preferred input to AIs but vision (images/animations/video) is the preferred output from them. Around a ~third of our brains are a massively parallel processor dedicated to vision, it is the 10-lane superhighway of information into brain. As AI improves, I think we'll see a progression: 1) raw text 2) markdown 3) HTML ... n) interactive neural videos/simulations

TLDR The input/output mind meld between humans and AIs is ongoing. Hot tip: try ask for HTML.

Anthropic Engineering: An update on recent Claude Code quality reports

Over the past month, we've been looking into reports that Claude's responses have worsened for some users. We've traced these reports to three separate changes that affected Claude Code, the Claude Agent SDK, and Claude Cowork. The API was not impacted. All three issues have now been resolved as of April 20 (v2.1.116).

Issue 1 (Mar 4): Changed default reasoning effort from high to medium. Reverted Apr 7.
Issue 2 (Mar 26): Bug in clearing thinking history for idle sessions caused Claude to appear forgetful. Fixed Apr 10.
Issue 3 (Apr 16): System prompt change to reduce verbosity hurt coding quality. Reverted Apr 20.
